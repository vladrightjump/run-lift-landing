# Run + Lift — equipment and event ideas

Date: 2026-10-04  
Context: Chișinău community, limited resources, approximately €2,000 considered for equipment, and a possible gym in 2027.

This captures the equipment and event discussion alongside the [product and website ideas](2026-10-04-run-lift-moldova-growth-ideation.md) and [digital-product evidence](2026-10-04-run-lift-digital-product-evidence.md). All capacities, prices and budgets below are proposals to test, not confirmed offers or forecasts.

## Starting point

User-reported facts:

- 12–20 people attend a session; around 90% pay.
- Existing equipment: 8 wall balls, 6 sandbags and around 10 kettlebells.
- Approximately €2,000 is being considered for additional equipment.
- The intended benefit is larger, more varied events that introduce new people to training.

Unknowns include unique monthly customers, current prices, equipment weights, available coaching hours, venue, transport and storage. Per-session attendance is not a count of unique members.

## Event concept: Run + Lift Team Challenge

A beginner-friendly experience where people join with a friend, learn the movements, complete a team challenge and receive their results.

Possible stations include running, sandbag work, kettlebell carries, wall balls where suitable targets exist, and bodyweight exercises. Offer appropriately adapted beginner and experienced options.

Rehearse with the current group. A 20–24-person wave is a proposed format to assess, not a verified capacity. If space, equipment, transitions and staffing work, two waves could accommodate 40–48 people in total. Adding waves can increase total attendance without requiring equipment for everyone at once.

Use rehearsal to identify queues, missing weight options, unclear instructions and time spent moving equipment. Buy against those observed needs.

## How the event can generate revenue

1. Paid event entries cover delivery and contribute toward equipment recovery.
2. Interested first-time attendees can buy a real four- or six-week training programme starting shortly afterwards.
3. Programme participants can continue into a defined monthly club package.
4. A proven format can later become a private company team experience.

Illustrative event economics:

| Item | Example only |
| --- | ---: |
| 40 paid entries × 200 MDL | 8,000 MDL revenue |
| Assumed delivery costs, including staff time | 5,000 MDL |
| Contribution before tax and equipment recovery | 3,000 MDL |

Neither the entry price nor the cost estimate has been validated. Count coach and organiser time, including the founders' time. Record membership revenue separately and avoid double-counting bundled purchases.

## Website ideas supporting the event

- An event page with the real date, venue, price, beginner options and wave availability.
- Individual or pair registration, with clear payment confirmation and attendance records.
- An explanation of what first-time participants should expect and bring.
- A results and photo experience, with participant permission for public sharing.
- A clear invitation into the next training programme after the event.
- A permanent training offer visible between event editions.

The existing registration and training pages provide a starting point. These additions are ideas; they have not been implemented or published.

## Proof to seek

Track the sequence:

**New person → paid event entry → actual attendance → paid training → renewal.**

Measure new attendees separately from existing members. A proposed first-pilot learning target is 10 new attendees and 3 training purchases within 14 days. This is a provisional test target, not statistical proof or a sufficient reason on its own to make a larger investment.

Also measure event contribution, coaching hours, equipment bottlenecks and participant experience. An event filled entirely by existing customers can generate revenue, but provides less evidence of customer acquisition.

## Connection to a possible gym in 2027

Events can grow relationships, reusable equipment and awareness. Test paid indoor sessions in an existing facility before committing to a lease. The relevant evidence is repeat purchases at a plausible location, timetable and price.

Calculate the required membership from actual quotes:

**Break-even members = monthly fixed costs ÷ monthly contribution per member.**

Include the full operating cost structure, and budget separately for startup equipment, fit-out and working capital. A suggested learning period is several repeated paid cycles; three profitable rented-space cycles would be informative, but would not prove that a dedicated gym is viable.

HYROX already has local training alternatives, so the brand proposition should emphasise an approachable start, familiar teammates and visible progress. [MARTS schedule](https://martsfitness.md/)

For an affiliated training club, obtain the actual applicable terms and costs. The official programme includes a route for existing facilities. [HYROX training-club programme](https://www.hyrox365.com/affiliation/hyrox-training-club)

## Recommended first experiment

Run one rehearsal and one paid pilot using the existing equipment. Connect the pilot to one clearly priced training programme. Keep the €700 reserve available until you know what limits attendance, delivery quality or revenue.

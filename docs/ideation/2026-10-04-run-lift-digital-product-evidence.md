# Sell a training system people can follow anywhere.

2026-10-04 · Updated direction: online assets, training programs and guided online experiences

There is evidence that people pay for digital fitness coaching. There is not yet evidence that your audience will buy a specific Run + Lift product. The useful next proof is a small paid pilot, with self-guided purchases measured separately from purchases of coach access.

## The first product I would test

Working concept · not yet for sale

### Run + Lift Foundations — six weeks of running + strength

**Buyer hypothesis:** Romanian-speaking recreational runners who can already do some running but do not know how to fit strength training into a consistent week. Start with people who recognize your coaching; include buyers who cannot attend your park sessions to test genuinely online demand.

**Promise to explore:** “Știi ce antrenament faci, cum îl execuți și cum continui săptămâna viitoare.” The product removes planning uncertainty. It should not promise a guaranteed race time or body transformation.

**Delivery:** a six-week calendar, your coach’s short demonstration videos, a simple progress log and clear guidance for choosing exercise variants and resuming after missed sessions. Customers train independently; the first version can be a downloadable pack plus a mobile-friendly resource page.

**Test price:** 399 MDL once for the self-guided version. This is an unvalidated starting hypothesis. A separate 899 MDL guided pilot could include the same assets plus six weekly group Q&A calls and explicitly limited group support. Those are two different purchases; measure them separately.

**Why it fits:** your project already publishes sequenced workouts and has real training footage, a coach/community story and a distribution audience. A complete paid syllabus, instructional video library and remote training experience are still proposed work.

**Tradeoff:** coach credibility, content quality, audience access and customer acquisition determine the business. Romanian language is a plausible differentiator to test, not a proven competitive advantage.

### The assets inside the flagship product

1. **Coach-reviewed weekly calendar:** purpose, schedule, session descriptions and progression.
2. **Instructional demonstrations:** setup, execution, common mistakes and suitable alternatives. Existing Instagram highlights are marketing footage, not automatically instruction.
3. **A usable training log:** completed sessions, effort and relevant performance notes.
4. **Decision guidance:** what to do after missing a session, which equipment version to follow and when to ask the coach.
5. **Start and finish review:** a coach-defined, appropriate comparison using consistent conditions, without implying a medical assessment.

Before taking payment, make the coach, intended level, equipment requirements, access period, delivery date and support limits clear. The training curriculum itself needs your qualified coach’s authorship/review.

## What would count as proof for Run + Lift?

A real purchase is the first demand signal. Use, completion and acceptable delivery costs are the next signals.

1. **Prepare one real sample.** Show one coach-approved week, one instructional video and the actual tracker. Add a concise overview of the full six-week product. Do not sell a vague future library.
2. **Present one self-guided offer at 399 MDL.** State whether it is a preorder, exactly when it will be delivered, and what happens if the pilot does not launch. Use a payment method available to your business. A form submission is not a purchase.
3. **Test with a reachable, relevant audience.** A starting discovery batch is thirty people fitting the buyer description, including people unable to attend in person. Record how many saw the offer, source, prior relationship, purchases, objections and refunds. No outreach has been sent.
4. **Use an explicit decision rule.** A proposed first gate is five full-price self-guided purchases, including at least two people outside the regular park group. This is a practical pilot threshold, not statistical proof or a universal conversion benchmark. Confirm that the revenue/cost commitment is acceptable before launching.
5. **Check delivery after purchase.** For a five-buyer pilot, aim for at least four to start week one and three to use the final week. These small-sample thresholds are provisional. Interview nonstarters, record refund reasons, and track support minutes rather than only asking whether they liked it.
6. **Test the next purchase.** Offer the next suitable block only when it is real and costed. Paid continuation or referrals from satisfied buyers strengthen the case for a recurring product; a second cohort reached outside the existing group tests distribution.

Interpret the outcome honestly

**Self-guided buyers:** initial evidence that the digital asset has value at the test price. **Only coached buyers:** evidence for a service-led online business; simplify, clarify or reposition the asset before treating it as validated. **Likes or email signups only:** interest, not willingness to pay. **No purchases:** examine audience fit, trust, sample quality and price before building more features.

Five purchases cannot establish national demand, scalable acquisition or profitability. Keep warm-community results separate from customers reached without a prior relationship.

<a id="economics"></a>

## Small economics test

Five buyers × 399 MDL = **1,995 MDL gross revenue**. Ten buyers = **3,990 MDL gross revenue**. That validates a purchase decision only; it may not repay production costs.

**Contribution per order = price − payment fees − expected refunds − delivery/support cost − acquisition cost.** Subtract one-time filming, editing and program creation costs separately. If you sell the guided version, cost every live call, check-in and moderation hour.

An illustrative calculation—not your actual costs: 399 − 12 payment fees − 25 refund allowance − 50 support/delivery − 100 acquisition = **212 MDL/order** before fixed costs and tax. At an assumed 3,000 MDL creation cost, approximately fifteen such orders cover that creation cost. Higher support or acquisition costs change the result quickly.

This is why a useful sample and a narrow buyer matter: they test trust and willingness to buy while keeping the initial content investment bounded.
